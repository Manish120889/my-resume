The Construction & Logistics
Reporting Audit Checklist

Answer 10 yes/no questions to find out how many hours your business is losing to manual reporting — and what automated reporting could look like for you.

⏱ Takes 3 minutes ✓ 10 questions Free — no email required
How to use this: Answer each question honestly. If you're unsure, answer based on how things work today — not how they should work. Five or more "No" answers usually means your business is spending 3–8 hours per week on tasks that could run automatically.
Section 1 — Data Collection
1
Do you compile reports manually from QuickBooks, Excel, or separate systems?
Manual compilation = the #1 time sink we see in construction and logistics businesses
2
Does your weekly or monthly reporting take more than 2 hours to produce?
Industry average for manual reporting: 4–6 hrs/week for a 10–30 person company
3
Do you see your job costs or delivery metrics updated in real time — or only after someone compiles the data?
Real-time = automated. After compilation = manual and always slightly stale
Section 2 — Visibility & Access
4
Does your team have to ask you (or someone else) for the numbers they need?
If people ask for data, reporting isn't self-serve — it's a bottleneck
5
Can you pull a report for any date range in under 2 minutes — without manual work?
Ad-hoc reporting speed reveals how much your data is structured vs. scattered
6
Do you track margins per project or route automatically — or do you calculate them manually?
Manual margin tracking often catches problems weeks after they happened
Section 3 — Automation & Alerts
7
Do you get an automatic alert if a job goes over budget or a delivery is delayed?
Without alerts, you find out about problems after they've already cost you money
8
Does your weekly summary report arrive without anyone compiling it?
If someone has to build the report — that time is a fixed weekly tax on your team
Section 4 — Accounting & Finance
9
Can your accountant or bookkeeper access the data they need without calling you?
Each accountant call-back for data typically costs 20–45 minutes of your time
10
Can you name your 3 most profitable jobs or routes right now — without opening a spreadsheet?
If the answer requires checking, your most important business insights aren't visible
