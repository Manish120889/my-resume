Common questions

Answering the hesitation before the call

Do I need a big system overhaul first?

No. We start with one focused reporting fix — not a full rebuild. Most clients see value within the first 2 weeks without changing any existing tools.

What if I do not know which service I need?

That is exactly what the free audit is for. We review your current workflow and recommend the clearest, most practical starting point.

Can this work with my existing spreadsheets?

Yes. We improve your current workflows — Excel, Google Sheets, QuickBooks, whatever you use. No forced platform changes. We work with what you have.

What if the issue is not a dashboard problem?

Then we will tell you. If there is no practical improvement to recommend, you do not proceed — and the audit costs you nothing.

How fast can I see value?

Typical first release is 2 weeks. Most clients see measurable time savings in the first month after delivery.

Is there a risk-free way to start?

The audit is free. The discovery call is free. Every project is fixed-price with clear scope. You know exactly what you are paying for before any work begins.
