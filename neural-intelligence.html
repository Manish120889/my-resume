Strategic Decision

Why It Was Archived

Neural Intelligence was built as the premium tier showcase for Surrey AI Automation — demonstrating the full capability ceiling of what the agency could deliver. After market testing with local Surrey businesses, the conversion data showed that prospects needed a simpler, more concrete entry point (Power BI dashboards, fixed-price, 2-week delivery) rather than a broad platform pitch. Neural Intelligence was archived as a future upsell product — fully functional, documented, and ready to reactivate as a retainer offering once the base client roster is established.
