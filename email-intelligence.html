Feature Set

What Was Built

📊

Inbox KPI Dashboard

Total emails, unique senders, peak hours, and response rate — updated live as new mail arrives via OAuth stream.

🗂️

AI Sender Categorisation

Every sender scored and labelled: Client, Vendor, Newsletter, Internal, or Noise — using LLM-assisted classification on sender metadata.

🕸️

Email Relationship Graph

Interactive node graph showing every contact and communication frequency. Filter by time window. Reveals hidden network patterns.

🌡️

Send-Time Heatmap

24-hour activity heatmap reveals when inbox is busiest. Used to optimise send timing, staff coverage, and SLA windows.

🔕

Unsubscribe Intelligence

Surfaces every newsletter and promo list ranked by volume. One-click audit of what's clogging the inbox with actionable cleanup.

🔒

Zero Data Architecture

Connects via OAuth read-only access. No emails stored on any server. All processing happens in the browser session only.

Strategic Decision

Why It Was Archived

Email Intelligence was production-deployed on Surrey AI Automation as a bundled feature with Power BI packages. After market testing, the decision was made to sharpen the site's focus exclusively on data analytics and Power BI — the strongest conversion driver. The Email Intelligence platform was archived to preserve it as a standalone product option for future deployment or licensing. All code, UI, and logic is retained in the project archive.
