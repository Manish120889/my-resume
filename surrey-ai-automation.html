Build Phases

Phase 1 — Cleanup

Remove non-core features, fix contact info, clean navigation

Archived and removed the Email Intelligence and Neural Intelligence sections that diluted the Power BI niche. Fixed phone number in JSON-LD and hero. Reduced nav from 9 items to 7 focused items.

Phase 2 — Readability

Background calibration and text-shadow system

Reduced video brightness from 0.55 → 0.32, max opacity from 1.0 → 0.38, added a 72% dark gradient overlay anchored to the hero section, and added text-shadow on all headlines for legibility against the space background.

Phase 3 — Content

Rewrite service pages for conversion

Coming — benefit-led rewrites of all three service pages, real social proof section, stronger About/Founder section with LinkedIn authority signals.

Phase 4 — Growth

Outreach scripts + LinkedIn content calendar

Coming — LinkedIn DM sequences for Surrey SMB owners, monthly content calendar, monitoring setup for uptime and form submissions.
